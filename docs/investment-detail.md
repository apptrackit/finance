# Holding details and direct-cash trade editor

The Investments page owns the selected holding ID, editor and mutation refresh. Holding details open in a centered single-column modal on all screen sizes, with sticky Buy/Sell actions for unit holdings. Manual total assets show ownership and Update value instead. Manually priced unit holdings retain their unit trades and their separate per-unit appraisal editor.

The holding popup follows reference **2a**: a centered 600px single-column panel with an instrument avatar, complete saved name, symbol/category/source-currency metadata, position value and compact total gain. Price history precedes a six-cell Your position grid: shares/units or ownership, native average cost, current price/valuation date, reporting cost basis, unrealized gain and realized gain. The prior gain bars and duplicate net-invested card are removed. Stock/ETF quantities use four displayed decimals with full precision in the title (tiny nonzero fractions and crypto retain up to 12); native prices use decimal-comma cents while sub-unit prices retain small-coin precision. Privacy also removes quantity titles and gain colors. Metadata is shown from saved/source facts; prototype names and ETF classifications are never inferred.

The compact 200px chart keeps real daily source data, split-adjusted prices, buy/sell markers, a dashed current average-cost reference and keyboard day selection. Its selected-day header replaces the range-change caption while hovering/selecting. A compatible, timed current quote may extend/replace only the chart endpoint on its source session date, with explicit provisional status; stale, unsupported, mistimed and other-currency quotes cannot overwrite the plotted daily history. The cache/source observations remain unchanged. Native daily behavior remains daily even though the prototype uses illustrative intraday curves. Source information appears after the position grid in plain words and readable local time, with refresh/retry retained. Trade history and legacy review remain available below; Buy/Sell actions stay fixed at the bottom.

## Detail reads and chart

Current quantity, native price/source time, reporting value, remaining average cost, net invested and gains come from the complete overview snapshot. Selecting a holding does not refetch its ledger: the overview already contains bounded, ordered trade facts. The panel shows 20 rows at a time, ordered by date, effective order and ID. Standalone legacy rows use the historical-details form. Versioned linked legacy unit trades with missing execution/cash facts expose Review historical details and use the canonical linked editor: its read verifies the reciprocal cash pair before opening, and its preview/save keep the original IDs and version checks. The editor read returns the verified linked cash amount/currency separately from stored investment facts. When the legacy investment cash field is missing, the draft prefills that recorded amount only if its currency matches the selected cash account; it never derives cash from execution price or current FX. Missing execution and fee facts must still be entered/confirmed, never inferred from a current quote. Broken/ambiguous pairs remain rejected by the server; locks, archive and privacy still disable review.

Instrument price history loads only when a market holding is opened with values visible. Its cache belongs to the App investment-data hook, is bounded to 12 entries and uses source snapshot/symbol/range keys. Returning to a matching detail reuses history; changed financial snapshots and mutations invalidate reuse. Superseded/closed reads abort; failed refreshes preserve the matching last good history. No financial history enters browser storage.

The holding price chart displays the retained provider split-only-adjusted daily close (`provider_close`), excluding dividend adjustment. The shared source cache/read contract still retains contemporaneous raw `close` for dated accounting. Every buy/sell in the selected range is a light/red marker at its actual date. A verified positive finite execution price in the chart currency is divided by all later split ratios through the current chart endpoint to set its display height; original trade prices and quantities remain unchanged in the ledger and dated list. Same-day split executions already use that day's share basis. Other-currency or unverified executions use hollow markers in a separate bottom lane: these represent dates only, never an inferred fill or a substituted nearby close. Their actual currency/facts remain in the tooltip and dated list. Marker selection opens the actual date; same-day trades remain separate records and all appear in the dated list/tooltip. The 1Y, 5Y and other range controls include all matching trades independently of daily-price sampling. A keyboard date selector and date readout expose every same-day execution. The range list shows at most the latest 100 executions; the ordered holding ledger remains separately paged in the panel. The dashed line is **current remaining native basis per unit**, explicitly not a historical cost series or an all-buys average. Range price change uses the first/last split-adjusted closes, including ranges with splits; it is not the holding's cash-flow return. The chart labels its split-adjusted basis. Normal prices display two decimal places, while sub-unit prices keep small-coin precision.

Manual histories remain dated total/per-unit step observations, independently loaded and paged. Their cost reference is current known remaining cost, not a deposit or new appraisal. Corrections pin the saved observation ID/version and retain drafts after failure. A successful appraisal refreshes the overview and account snapshots without cash movement.

## Transfer entry points

New buys and sells start on Investments. The Dashboard transaction composer offers only cash accounts for income, expense and both transfer sides; saved investment-account defaults are ignored. Existing linked trade rows can still open the canonical versioned editor for corrections.

Editing an existing investment transfer first loads the canonical reciprocal pair and saved version, then opens the same editor. Reads abort when superseded/unmounted; stale versions or invalid pairs reject opening. Save/delete/preview/uncertain retry and refresh ownership are the canonical editor's existing paths. The composer no longer has an investment price/fee form or its own investment save implementation. Cash-to-cash transfers, split transactions and MCP review transfers remain separate; existing cash transfers cannot be retargeted into a holding.

## Recording and replacing trades

Record trade on Investments or Buy/Sell in a unit holding opens the dedicated editor. Its saved values are string drafts using AmountInput. It collects the holding, exact units, execution price/currency/date, cash account, included fee and actual settlement. Same-currency cash must equal rounded gross plus the buy fee or minus the sale fee. Cross-currency settlement is entered and explicitly confirmed; displayed current FX is only an estimate. Included fees never create another expense. The existing negative-cash policy remains a warning; overselling is rejected locally and by full API timeline replay.

Quotes never populate execution facts automatically. Use as draft is available only for today's date, requires confirmation of the actual fill, and clears a quote-derived price if the date or currency changes. Past fills require their recorded execution. Manual total-asset events use exactly one ITEM; appraisals remain a separate action. A confirmed zero-proceeds whole-asset disposal is supported without treating a zero appraisal as missing ownership.

A debounced nonmutating preview uses the same API planner/accounting engine as saving. The editor waits for the matching preview, shows authoritative native cash balance after, resulting units/basis and the specific sale gain, and revalidates after source revision changes. API saving always rechecks locks, archives and the whole timeline; a preview is not a reservation. Only successful writes close the editor and refresh shared account/overview/history data. Failed saves retain all fields and the same request key for unchanged retries. An uncertain response pauses editing and hides a prospective second-trade preview; Retry original save recovers the original result (or applies that request once), even if refreshed units/version/locks would block a new trade. A structured 4xx rejection permits corrections with a fresh key. Unchanged recovery is blocked after the 24-hour receipt window, and closing explains that the retry key will be lost and history must be checked. In-flight submissions and duplicate clicks are guarded.

## Pair preparation and deletion

`GET /investment-trades/:id` accepts the investment or cash ID, resolves the reciprocal pair and returns `{trade, cash_account_id, source_revision}` with `Cache-Control: no-store`. It verifies links, posted/date/sign/settlement agreement, active/unlocked accounts and an unchanged revision. It creates no financial rows, audits or receipts. It never infers a missing historical execution currency, fee or cash contribution. The client checks the row version and overview revision before opening the editor and pins that edit snapshot through later refreshes.

`PUT /investment-trades/:id` retains the existing complete replacement/expected-version/request-key contract. Replacements reverse the original legs before applying new facts, including when the cash account changes. The dedicated editor does not move an edit onto another holding.

`DELETE /investment-trades/:id?expected_version=N` checks the editor's saved version before reversing the linked pair. A concurrent edit invalidates the D1 claim; a retry with an older version is refused. Malformed versions fail validation. Existing callers without this optional query retain their previous behavior; the new editor always supplies it. Deletion still replays later events, guards both accounts and reverses each ledger once.

Privacy hides values, quantities, costs, fees, percentages, charts and execution descriptions; reveal is required for mutation. Dirty drafts protect Back/navigation/reload and close/discard actions. Dialogs trap focus, confirmation Escape cancels only the confirmation, failures focus their inline feedback and successful appraisals restore Update value focus. Both themes and reduced-motion chart behavior use app controls/tokens.

This step adds no schema migration, durable table, financial browser preference or MCP investment write. Final whole-epic acceptance and fresh/upgrade release validation remain #135; main and production are unchanged until separately authorized.
